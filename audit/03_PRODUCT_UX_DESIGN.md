## PROTECTED DESIGN ELEMENTS

Before any UI change, verify it passes the HNWI test:
- Would a client of Sotheby's, Knight Frank, or Savills consider this premium?
- Is the font legible at all screen sizes?
- Does the gold palette communicate luxury?
- Is the spacing generous (luxury = whitespace)?
- Are all interactive elements accessible via keyboard?

If any answer is NO — do not ship.
