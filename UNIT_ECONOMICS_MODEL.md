## WHAT MUST BE TRACKED FROM DEAL 1

Every closed deal must record:
- Lead source (how did they find us?)
- First contact date
- Days to close
- Number of viewings
- Reason selected Agency Group
- Would they refer?
- Co-broker involved?
- Total cost to close

*This data makes every future deal cheaper and faster.*
