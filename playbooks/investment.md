## FINANCIAL CALCULATIONS (must be correct)
- Gross yield = Annual rent / Purchase price
- Net yield = (Annual rent - OpEx) / Purchase price
- NOI = Revenue - Operating Expenses
- Cash-on-cash = Annual cash flow / Equity invested
- IRR = Internal rate of return (levered and unlevered)
- Equity multiple = Total distributions / Equity invested

## MANDATORY DISCLAIMERS
Every investment projection must state:
- "Past performance does not guarantee future results"
- "This is not personalised investment advice"
- "Projections are illustrative only"

## SCENARIOS (always show all three)
- DOWNSIDE: conservative assumptions
- BASE: realistic assumptions
- UPSIDE: optimistic assumptions

## NEVER
- Present projected returns as guaranteed
- Use investment terminology carelessly
- Show IRR without showing assumptions
- Allow AI to make binding investment recommendations
- Publish returns without legal review
